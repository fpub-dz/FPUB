Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

Most engagements run 6 to 12 weeks from discovery to launch, depending on scope. We share a detailed timeline at the end of the strategy phase.

Do you work with startups or enterprises?

Both. Our process scales from lean MVP builds for early-stage startups to multi-market rollouts for established enterprises.

What is your technology stack?

We favor Next.js and React on the front end, headless commerce for storefronts, and cloud-native infrastructure tuned to each client's scale.

Will we own the intellectual property?

Yes. Upon final payment, full ownership of the code, design files, and assets we create transfers to you.
